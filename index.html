    MorphoTrace — help

    Draw mode (D)

    • Click add a vertex
    • Shift+drag trace freehand
    • Click the first vertex, Enter or double-click to close; a polygon closed over an existing one is merged into it
    • Right-click or Backspace: remove last vertex · Esc cancel

    Edit mode (E)

    • Click a polygon to select it
    • Drag a vertex to move it
    • Double-click a contour line to split it with a new vertex at that spot
    • Hover a vertex (it grows) and right-click, or Alt+click, to delete it; its neighbours are joined
    • H toggle hole · Del delete polygon

    View

    • Shift+wheel: zoom at cursor · F fit
    • Wheel (vertical / horizontal): scroll the image
    • Space+drag or right-drag: pan
    • ←/→ previous / next image
    • X mark image unusable (with reason)

    Measurement

    Area is computed exactly from the polygon vertices (shoelace formula, holes subtracted) and also from the rasterised binary mask. Physical area uses the scale field: mm² = px × (µm/px)² / 10⁶.
